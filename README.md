# Oval: an Olin community mockup

Oval is an interactive mockup of a community app for the Olin College of Engineering community: students, alumni, faculty and staff.

**Try it:** https://pranav-bonthu.github.io/oval-mockup/

What you can click through:

- **Home:** a community feed of projects, wins, startups, events and school news, with reactions, comments and "reach out" buttons. A "Preview as" switch shows the student and alumni versions (alumni see school news first, then the groups they follow).
- **Your corner of Olin:** a small web of the people who share a club, class or company with you.
- **Oliners:** search everyone by name, skill, company or club, and filter by role, what they're open to, industry and location.
- **Profiles:** their connections, how you're connected to them, posts they're in, and a reach-out or intro request flow.
- **Messages:** an inbox for messaging other Oliners one on one.

Every person, post and message is fictional sample data. Nothing is saved; refreshing the page resets it.

Feedback welcome: open an issue or message Pranav.

Built as a single static page with [Vue 3](https://vuejs.org) (MIT license, included as `vue.global.prod.js`).
